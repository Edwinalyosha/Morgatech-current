Is Your Suspension Trying to Tell You Something?

Don't ignore the warning signs! A compromised suspension system affects more than just ride comfor; it severely impacts steering, braking, and tire life. If you're experiencing any of the following, it’s time for an inspection:

  • Bouncy Ride: Excessive vertical movement or bouncing after hitting bumps or uneven surfaces.
  • Swaying & Leaning: Your car sways excessively when taking turns, or visibly leans to one side.
  • Noises Over Bumps: Hearing persistent clunking, knocking, or creaking noises from under the car.
  • Steering Issues: The vehicle pulls to one side when driving straight, or steering feels loose or unresponsive.
